# 3-2-3-3 result-fullscene-v5 prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used once per state. Each generation used the previous bridge result only as a composition reference and the approved transparent `zfa-03-sudalmon` image only as a character-identity reference. The final scene itself is generated as one native raster image; no character cutout is composited afterward.

Shared constraints for all six prompts:

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset type: 1280×800 educational game final reward scene
- Keep the whole reward bridge and Sudalmon on the left two-thirds.
- Render Sudalmon as a large, full-body participant standing on the bridge, with scene-matched perspective, contact shadow, reflected light, and natural occlusion. It must not look pasted on.
- Reserve the right side for one blank dark-navy result board with a warm gold double-line frame, approximately x=860, y=135, w=380, h=590.
- No text, numerals, letters, logos, icons, progress bars, buttons, or extra UI inside or outside the board.
- Keep the board and the main bridge from overlapping.
- Bright polished 2D anime game illustration; child-friendly; crisp warm outlines; no photorealism and no 3D toy look.
- One Sudalmon only, exactly two arms and two paws, complete body visible, no duplicated limbs or detached accessories.
- The six scenes must read from humble to legendary through bridge construction, light, particles, materials, and Sudalmon reaction. Adjacent stages differ in at least two of those dimensions.
